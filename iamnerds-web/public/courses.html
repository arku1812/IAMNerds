Frequently asked questions

Are these official vendor courses?

No. IAMNerds courses are independent training written by practitioners. Product names are used only to describe what the course covers.

Do I need my own licence for the labs?

No. Each course comes with a lab environment: vendor trial or developer tenants where they exist, and prepared virtual machines for on-premises products.

Are there live classes?

Courses are self-paced. Optional live instructor-led batches run for selected courses; join the waitlist to hear about dates.

Can I train a whole team?

Yes. Get in touch and we will put together courses, paths and live sessions for your team.

Not sure where to start?

Take the free IAM Fundamentals course, or tell us your role and we will suggest a path.
