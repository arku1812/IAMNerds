Industries

Identity security for every sector.

Wherever sensitive data and critical systems need protecting, access has to be right. Every industry has its own identity challenges. These are the ones we work in.

Automotive

Plant, dealer and supplier access across global networks, with SAP at the core.

  • Supplier and dealer identities
  • SAP role provisioning across plants
  • TISAX-relevant access controls

Aviation

Shift-based crews, contractors and safety-critical systems that can't wait for access.

  • Fast onboarding for crews and contractors
  • Emergency and break-glass access
  • Clear audit trails for regulated systems

Banking & Financial Services

Segregation of duties, recertification cycles and regulator-ready evidence.

  • SoD rules and conflict checks
  • Regular access recertification
  • Evidence for regulators and auditors

Pharmaceuticals

Validated systems and clean audit trails for regulated research and production data.

  • Access to GxP-validated systems
  • Documented approval workflows
  • Traceable changes to entitlements

Retail

High staff turnover across stores, with seasonal joiners and leavers every month.

  • Automated joiner and leaver processes
  • Store and role-based access
  • Seasonal staff without lingering accounts

Telecom

Privileged network access and thousands of service accounts behind customer operations.

  • Privileged access to network infrastructure
  • Service account ownership at scale
  • NIS2-relevant controls

Tourism

Seasonal staff, partner portals and booking systems shared across many hands.

  • Partner and external user access
  • Seasonal onboarding and offboarding
  • Shared accounts replaced by personal ones

Insurance

Agents, brokers and claims systems that handle sensitive personal data.

  • Access for agents and brokers
  • Least privilege on customer data
  • Recertification for sensitive systems

Manufacturing

IT meets OT: shared shop-floor accounts, machine identities and plant systems.

  • Shared shop-floor accounts brought under control
  • Machine and OT identities
  • Plant system access by role
