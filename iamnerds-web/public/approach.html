Approach

How a project runs.

Four phases, each with a clear outcome. Small enough to start fast, structured enough to survive a steering committee.

01

Discover

Inventory every identity and entitlement, human and machine. Find the orphans and the over-privileged, and understand the processes behind them.

Outcome

  • Identity and entitlement inventory
  • List of risks and quick wins
  • Agreed scope for the next phase
02

Design

Roles, policies, approval flows and the target architecture, agreed with the business, not just with IT.

Outcome

  • Target architecture and data flows
  • Role model and approval workflows
  • Implementation plan with milestones
03

Build

Implement the platform, connect HR, SAP and directories, and automate the identity lifecycle, in increments you can test.

Outcome

  • Configured identity platform
  • Connected source and target systems
  • Automated joiner, mover and leaver processes
04

Govern

Certification campaigns, monitoring and a clean handover to your operations team, so the system keeps working after we leave.

Outcome

  • First certification campaigns completed
  • Operations documentation and runbooks
  • Trained administrators
