About

Nerds, on purpose.

IAMNerds is an online training platform purely for Identity & Access Management technologies, based in Germany and open to learners worldwide.

Who teaches

IAMNerds was founded by an IAM/IGA architect with more than 18 years of hands-on enterprise identity projects, from greenfield rollouts to rescuing implementations that had stalled.

The courses teach what those projects taught: how the platforms really behave, where they break and how to design them so they still work when the auditors arrive.

How we teach

A few principles behind every course.

NEUTRAL

Vendor-neutral

Independent courses for every major platform, so you learn the concepts that carry over, not just one product's menus.

HANDS-ON

Labs, not slides

Every module ends in a lab in a real environment, with a worked solution when you get stuck.

REAL

Project scenarios

Messy HR feeds, slow job queues and audit findings, solved the way they are solved on real projects.

PROVEN

Reviewed capstones

Your capstone is reviewed by an instructor who has built the same thing for real clients.
