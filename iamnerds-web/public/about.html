About

Nerds, on purpose.

IAMNerds is an Identity & Access Management consultancy based in Germany, working with organizations worldwide, remotely or on site.

Who we are

IAMNerds was founded by an IAM/IGA architect with more than 18 years of hands-on enterprise identity projects, from greenfield rollouts to rescuing implementations that had stalled.

We are based in Germany and work with organizations worldwide, remotely or on site, in English and German. We like clean role models, readable documentation and audit findings that never happen.
