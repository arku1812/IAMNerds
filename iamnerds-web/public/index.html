Non-human identity & AI agents

Not every identity has a pulse.

Machine identities now outnumber people in most environments, and AI agents are joining them fast. They log in, call APIs and touch production data. We govern them with the same discipline as your employees.

  • OwnerEvery service account and agent has a named, accountable human.
  • PurposeAccess is tied to a documented job, not to "it might need it".
  • ScopeLeast privilege by default, with secrets vaulted and rotated.
  • ExpiryCredentials and permissions end when the job does.

How a project runs

Four phases, each with a clear outcome. Small enough to start fast, structured enough to survive a steering committee.

  1. Discover

    Inventory every identity and entitlement, human and machine. Find the orphans and the over-privileged.

  2. Design

    Roles, policies, approval flows and the target architecture, agreed with the business.

  3. Build

    Implement the platform, connect HR, SAP and directories, and automate the lifecycle.

  4. Govern

    Certification campaigns, monitoring and a clean handover to your operations team.
