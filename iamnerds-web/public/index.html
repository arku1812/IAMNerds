How learning works

The same rhythm in every course, so you always know what comes next.

  1. Learn

    Short videos of 8–15 minutes, a cheat sheet per module and a real-world scenario solved step by step.

  2. Lab

    Hands-on exercises in a prepared environment, with a step-by-step guide and a worked solution.

  3. Build

    An end-to-end capstone project, reviewed by an instructor who has done it for real.

  4. Prove it

    A final exam with a practical task and a certificate you can link on LinkedIn.

Non-human identity & AI agents

Not every identity has a pulse.

Machine identities now outnumber people, and AI agents are joining them fast. Our vendor-neutral course teaches you to govern them with the same discipline as employees.

  • OwnerEvery service account and agent has a named, accountable human.
  • PurposeAccess is tied to a documented job, not to "it might need it".
  • ScopeLeast privilege by default, with secrets vaulted and rotated.
  • ExpiryCredentials and permissions end when the job does.
