Services

Identity work, done properly.

Eight areas where we help organizations get access right: from the first access inventory to the last certification campaign, and everything that has to keep working when the auditors arrive.

STRATEGY

IGA strategy & architecture

Before anyone configures a connector, you need to know where you are going. We assess your current identity landscape, define a target architecture and help you choose a platform that fits your systems, your team and your budget.

  • Current-state assessment of identities, systems and processes
  • Target architecture and vendor-neutral platform selection
  • A phased roadmap your teams can actually deliver
LIFECYCLE

Joiner, mover, leaver automation

When HR data drives provisioning, access arrives on day one, changes when people change roles and disappears on their last day. No tickets, no forgotten accounts.

  • HR system as the authoritative source for identities
  • Automated provisioning and deprovisioning to target systems
  • Clean handling of contractors, rehires and transfers
ROLES

Role models & access certifications

Business roles people understand, and access reviews that are more than rubber stamps. We build role models from real entitlement data and design certification campaigns that reviewers can finish.

  • Role mining and business role design
  • Segregation-of-duties rules
  • Certification campaigns with sensible scope and reminders
PAM

Privileged access management

Admin accounts vaulted, sessions recorded and standing privileges reduced to as close to zero as you can bear. We connect PAM with your identity governance so privileged access is requested, approved and reviewed like everything else.

  • Discovery of privileged and shared accounts
  • Vaulting, rotation and session management
  • Just-in-time access instead of permanent admin rights
NHI

Non-human identities

Service accounts, API keys, secrets and workload identities usually outnumber employees. We find them, give each one a named owner and bring them under the same governance as people. More on non-human identities →

  • Inventory of service accounts, keys and secrets
  • Ownership, purpose and expiry for every machine identity
  • Secret rotation and vaulting
AI AGENTS

AI agent identity governance

AI agents log in, call APIs and act on data. They need scoped, time-bound access with an accountable human behind every one, and an audit trail that shows what they did.

  • Identity model for AI agents and their delegated access
  • Least-privilege scopes and short-lived credentials
  • Logging and review of agent activity
INTEGRATION

SAP & directory integration

SAP, Active Directory and Entra ID connected cleanly to your identity platform, so roles, accounts and groups stay in sync without manual work.

  • SAP ABAP, SAP Cloud Identity Services and role provisioning
  • Active Directory and Entra ID group and account management
  • Hybrid on-premises and cloud scenarios
COMPLIANCE

Audit readiness

Evidence on demand for ISO 27001, NIS2 and internal audit, without the Excel archaeology. We map controls to what your identity platform actually does and make the evidence easy to produce.

  • Control mapping for ISO 27001, NIS2 and internal policies
  • Reports for who has access to what, and why
  • Remediation of audit findings

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with a specific IGA vendor?

No. We are vendor-neutral and work with One Identity Manager, SailPoint, Omada, Saviynt and other platforms. We recommend what fits your landscape.

Can you take over an implementation that has stalled?

Yes. We review what has been built, identify what is blocking progress and agree a realistic plan to get the project back on track.

Do you work remotely or on site?

Both. We work with clients worldwide, mostly remotely, and on site when workshops or go-lives need it.
